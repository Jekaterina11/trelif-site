Frequently asked questions

Where is my TreLif data stored?

Primarily locally on the user's device.

Does TreLif require an account?

No, the current version does not require an online TreLif account.

Does TreLif use Apple Health / HealthKit?

No, not in the current version.

How can I delete my data?

Me → Privacy & Data → Delete All My Data.

Does TreLif contain advertising?

No, the current version does not contain third-party advertising.

For more details, read the TreLif Privacy Policy.
